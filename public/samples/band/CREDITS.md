# Backing band recordings

Built by `scripts/build-band-samples.py` (trimmed, mixed and encoded to MP3) from:

- **Piano:** Splendid Grand Piano (Steinway), public domain. https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SplendidGrandPiano
- **Upright bass:** 1958 Otto Rubner double bass, pizzicato, played and mapped by D. Smolken, CC0. https://github.com/sfzinstruments (via https://github.com/danigb/samples)
- **Electric bass:** "Electric Bass (finger)" from the FluidR3 GM soundfont by Frank Wen, CC BY 3.0, as packaged in https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
- **Drums:** Virtuosity Drums, a jazz club kit played by Austin McMahon, recorded at Virtuosity Musical Instruments (Boston) by Versilian Studios, CC0. https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums
