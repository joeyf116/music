Privacy policy

Fretboard Practice · last updated 31 October 2026

Fretboard Practice is a guitar practice tool. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never asks for your name, email, location, contacts or microphone.

What stays on your device

Your settings (key, scale, tuning, tempo, sounds), starred resources, quiz scores and charts you have not shared are saved in the app's local storage on your device only. Deleting the app, or clearing site data in your browser, removes them.

What you choose to share

If you add a practice resource or share a chord chart, the text you enter (for example a title, link, tags or chord symbols) is stored in a database hosted by Supabase and is visible to everyone who uses the app. A random code is kept on your device so that only you can edit or remove what you shared. Reports of spam or broken entries store only the reason you type. Please don't put personal information in shared entries.

Like any website, the servers that deliver the app (GitHub Pages) and the shared catalog (Supabase) may briefly log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service running. We don't use that data to identify you.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Removing shared content or contacting us

Use the remove or report buttons in the app, or open an issue at github.com/joeyf116/music/issues.
